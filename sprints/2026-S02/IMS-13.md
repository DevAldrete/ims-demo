## Context

Deliver an intuitive dock-side receiving screen allowing warehouse staff to quickly verify PO line items, input received counts, and flag discrepancies.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Responsive receiving dashboard showing open POs filtered by supplier and expected date
- [ ] Dynamic line-item grid with visual indicators: green (exact match), yellow (partial), red (over-received or damaged)
- [ ] Modal dialog to log vendor delivery note number, carrier tracking, and upload packing slip photo
- [ ] Print receipt confirmation and putaway routing sheet
