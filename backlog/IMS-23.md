## Context

Give CFOs and Operations Executives visual insight into inventory capital investment, aging inventory (>90/180/365 days), and turnover ratios.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Charts showing total inventory valuation calculated via FIFO and Weighted Average Cost
- [ ] Stock aging bucket visualization (0-30 days, 31-90 days, 91-180 days, >180 days)
- [ ] Dead-stock identification report for obsolete items
- [ ] Exportable PDF and Excel financial audit summaries
